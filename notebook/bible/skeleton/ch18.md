### Ch18 — The Hilt of Tyr
**POV:** Darneil (first half — the rail, the sound of them coming), then Felix.
**Day/time:** D5 Mon, 1:30–6 pm. **Locations:** the river way; the rail; the
hold.

**ENTRY** = Ch17 EXIT.

**Purpose:** Father. The Hilt on the altar stone. The protectors, in a crypt,
as talk: the French, the lost hilt, the chapel's keepers, "Tyr gave his hand to
bind the wolf, they say." What fourteen years made of him: the wall of dates,
the height marks — Felix stands against the last one and is shorter than his
father guessed. The knot in the cord: "I tied that. In the rain." Darneil asks
to see it; it does not leave Felix's hand. The first letter opened and read,
haltingly, in Darneil's translation: what she wrote to a newborn. The ring on
the cord — Darneil gives it to Felix. "Mrs. Walker" = Janet, in the room.

**EXIT**
- Necklace: on Felix. Her signet ring now on the same cord, beside the
  obsidian.
- Folder & letters: in the hold, on the altar step. First letter opened and
  read; second and third sealed.
- Bike & helmets: in the lot.
- Injuries: Felix's knee; Addie's wrist.
- Felix knows: his father; the Hilt and what it does (the shadow will not come
  past the rail); the first letter; the charm's full rule (kept / worn / off;
  the ward; why the Knights' house was silence); that his father cannot leave
  the rail; that Darneil has never once in fourteen years said her name and
  will not now.
- Addie knows: the same; and that Felix looked at his father's hands before
  his face.
- Mistress knows: the boy is behind the rail with the Delvaux man and the
  Hilt. She has what she needed: he is *there*. Tonight she will go to the
  rail with the kept ones.
- Darneil knows: his son's face; the whisper (Felix told him); that she has
  been up top by day in an apron — which she has never done; that she is
  coming.
- Janet knows: she and Alcide will have to hold the door from above if this
  goes wrong; Alcide has a truck.
- Others: Anatole went back to the hall at 4 and found Armand gone and six men
  gone with him.
