### Ch07 — The Man Who Made the Necklace
**POV:** Felix. **Day/time:** D2 Fri, 12:20–2:15 pm. **Locations:** the pawn
shop counter; the front window; Main Street; the bike.

**ENTRY** = Ch06 EXIT.

**Purpose:** It is a concealment charm; "a quiet thing in a loud room"; put it
on (the inserted beat); the seal; "you are hers"; no name in a room with the
door unlocked; the orphanage lot; the flinch at *mom*; go home a different way;
three locks and a chain; the unicorn turns and points at Felix. He does not
take it off.

**EXIT — the end of Act I. Complete state.**
- Necklace: on Felix, under his shirt, since Alcide made him put it on at
  about 12:35. Not off again until Ch18 (in his own hand) and Ch21 (on
  purpose).
- Folder & letters: Felix folded the three sealed letters back into the
  folder at the counter; the folder is in the bag on Addie's bike. Contents:
  papers; three sealed letters (her mark); the opened envelope with the note.
  Nobody has opened a letter.
- Bike & helmets: Addie and Felix on the bike, both helmeted, leaving Main
  Street "a different way" (she takes the river road home to drop him — which
  is how she notices, Friday, that Riverbend is on the way).
- Injuries: none.
- Felix knows: the necklace hides him ("as long as this is on you, you are
  hard to find … take it off and you are a candle in a dark house"); Alcide
  made it for a woman who wanted to be gone; the letters carry her seal; "you
  are hers"; the woman "had been" at the orphanage; the lot is Riverbend; go in
  daytime; the necklace stays on; the whisper said the man would say this and
  that he is wrong; Alcide looked at his ears.
- Addie knows: everything Felix knows except the whisper; her mother looked at
  a unit at Riverbend; something crossed the man's face at *mom*; the unicorn
  turned with no wind; she does not like Main Street.
- Mistress knows: Alcide sent them to the lot, not home — so Alcide is one of
  *them*; the boy kept it on; the girl saw the unicorn turn; tonight she will
  go to the river-road drain and wait under the lot.
- Darneil knows: nothing yet. (Word reaches him Sunday morning.)
- Janet knows: nothing yet of the last two hours; Alcide will call her at 2:30
  and say "Come by"; she will go to him at 7 and down the wooden door at 11 to
  tell Anatole: the boy has the box, has been to Alcide, is coming to the lot;
  tell Delvaux; do not present him.
- Others: Alcide has locked three locks and a chain and is standing in his
  back room with his hands shaking. Anatole will know by midnight. Armand has
  been at the lot since Wednesday, watching, on her word.
- Weather: warm, still, no wind; clouds by evening.
