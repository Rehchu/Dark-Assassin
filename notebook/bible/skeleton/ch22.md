### Ch22 — Mother
**POV:** Felix (to the low table), then Leila. **Day/time:** D6 Tue 10 pm – D7
Wed 1 am. **Locations:** the river way; the market square; a fissure; the
shrouded room; back.

**ENTRY** = Ch21 EXIT.

**Purpose:** The emotional core. He walks to her with the necklace in his
pocket and the ring on his neck. She does not touch him. The name in the brass
box: *Cyprien*. The lock of hair. The third letter — she knows it by heart and
says it to him, and it is the most tender thing in the book and it is a
hunter's letter. What she wants: him, down here, with her, learning to let it;
the not-being-seen filled; a son. What she offers: the three kept ones, the
town left alone, Addie left alone. He is torn — the reader must believe he
might. He refuses. He takes the necklace out of his pocket and puts it on in
front of her, not joking. "How does it look." She lets him go because she has
what she came for: she can find him anywhere now. She gives him the three as
"a mother's gift" and says the one true thing: "I told you I'd hear you."
Then her POV for a page — the ache, quiet — and: "Up top, then."

**EXIT**
- Necklace: ON Felix, since 12:40 am, put on in front of her. Its hiding is
  broken for her and only for her.
- Folder & letters: in the hold. Felix now knows what the third letter says;
  it is still sealed.
- Bike & helmets: in the lot.
- Injuries: as Ch21; Addie's slowing worn off, hand numb; Darneil awake and
  the same.
- Positions: Felix walking back up the river way with three strangers on
  stretchers carried by Armand's two abandoned men, whom she gave him too.
  The hold as Ch21. The Mistress in the shrouded room; Armand on her floor,
  her creature now. Alcide in the hold since 10, sitting with Darneil, both of
  them afraid of the same woman.
- Felix knows: his birth name; the third letter; that she will be up top; that
  he said no and meant it and that saying no was the first thing in the book
  he decided alone.
- Addie knows: he came back; he is wearing it; his hands are cold; she is not
  going to ask what was said tonight and he is going to tell her anyway, on
  the stair.
- Mistress knows: where he is at every moment, now, the way you know where
  your own hand is. His name up top. That the Hilt is going up with him.
- Darneil knows: the boy came back with it on; the Hilt passes in the morning.
- Janet knows: they go up at first light by the door; Alcide's truck; the
  hospital story ("found him wandering by the river road").
- Others: Anatole will hold the door again, with fewer people. Armand goes up
  top with her when she goes.
