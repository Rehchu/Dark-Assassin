### Ch06 — The Pawn Shop
**POV:** Felix. **Day/time:** D2 Fri, 11:50 am–12:20 pm. **Locations:** the
bike (Third to Main); the pawn shop front; inside, the counter.

**ENTRY** = Ch05 EXIT.

**Purpose:** The ride he never wants to take again. The closed shop and the
figure in the window. Trinkets, books, clothes, the unicorn. The craftsman
examines the *string* first — a knot he did not tie — then every bead, then
the stone, and is mesmerized; then the anger that is fear. The necklace
handed over: off his neck and on a stranger's counter — the point of the scene
is that the maker recognizes his own work.

**EXIT**
- Necklace: on the counter in Alcide's hands, about to go onto a square of
  felt under a lamp. Kept, arm's reach.
- Folder & letters: in the bag on the bike outside — no: Addie brought the
  bag in; the folder is on her lap on the stool she is about to be told to sit
  on.
- Bike & helmets: bike at the Main Street curb; both helmets on the counter's
  end.
- Injuries: none. Felix nauseous.
- Felix knows: the craftsman knows the necklace and is angry; "it was in a box
  of some old stuff my parents left me."
- Addie knows: someone moved in the window before they knocked; the shop was
  closed and opened for them; "Of course we do! It's why we are here!"
- Mistress knows: they are in Alcide's shop; she is in the doorway across Main
  Street with the shadow thin as paper in the noon light.
- Darneil knows: as before.
- Janet knows: as before.
- Others: Alcide knows exactly who is standing at his counter.
