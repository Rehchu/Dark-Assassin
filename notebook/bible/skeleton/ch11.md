**Locations:** the hold — the crypt, the altar, the rail, the wall of dates.

**Purpose:** Fourteen years in one room. The Hilt seen for the first time. The
charcoal dates; the height marks; her ring on his cord. Anatole crosses the

"Armand won't hold to that." Darneil: "Then you'll have to."
