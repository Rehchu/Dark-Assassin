### Ch13 — Followed
**POV:** Addie. **Day/time:** D4 Sun, 4:30–8 pm. **Locations:** the foot of the
bell-stair; the old street; a side vault; the hall's outer door.

**ENTRY** = Ch12 EXIT.

**Purpose:** They are not alone. Addie feels them before she sees them
(weather). Followed. Then taken — a net across the street, hoods, hands that are
frightened and not cruel — by Anatole's watchers, minutes before Armand's men
reach the same place. Ti-Jean says the first kind word. Phones dead. The
backpack taken. The door above is barred from below. Point of no return: the
book will not go up again until Wednesday.

**EXIT — the end of Act II. Complete state.**
- Necklace: on Felix. (The watchers searched them and did not touch it; Odile
  said not to.)
- Folder & letters: in Addie's backpack, in Anatole's keeping in the hall. Not
  opened.
- Bike & helmets: in the Riverbend lot, in the rain.
- Injuries: Felix — a knee banged hard on stone going down in the net; he
  limps through Ch14–16 and it bruises black. Addie — a rope-burned right
  wrist. Nothing else.
- Phones: dead (no signal; Addie's at 9%, Felix's at 40% and used as a
  flashlight).
- Felix knows: there are people down here, a lot of them; they knew he was
  coming; a boy said "you're the one"; they have Addie's pack with the folder.
- Addie knows: she felt them coming a full minute before Felix did; the
  people are afraid of something that is not them; there is a second group
  (she saw sunglasses in lamplight) that the first group hurried them away
  from; her mother has been here (Ti-Jean: "You're Miss Jan's girl").
- Mistress knows: they are down; Anatole's people got them first; Armand
  failed her by minutes; she has time; the truce says they will be presented.
- Darneil knows: at 7 pm Ti-Jean ran to the rail: "We have them. Anatole says
  tonight, after he's talked to them." He is standing at the rail and will
  not sit.
- Janet knows: on shift 7 pm–7 am; Addie is under the city; Anatole has her;
  she wrote the time on her wrist again.
- Others: Anatole has them in the hall's outer room; Armand has come into the
  hall angry and is saying the truce's third term out loud where everyone can
  hear it. Alcide is asleep over the shop with a chair against the door.
- Weather: rain, heavy, all night.
