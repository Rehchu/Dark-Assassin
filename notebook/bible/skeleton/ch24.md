### Ch24 — She Knows Where He Is
**POV:** Felix. **Day/time:** D8 Thu, Thanksgiving, 9 am–3 pm. **Locations:**
Rapides Regional, room 412; the Knights' house — the table with the leaf in,
the alphabetized spice rack; the front window; the street.

**ENTRY** = Ch23 EXIT.

**Purpose:** The close. Morning at the hospital: Darneil, thick-tongued, names
the second piece — "There's a second. The Blade. It went up the river when the
hilt came down it. Natchitoches. The old fort on the Cane. That's all my
father knew and all I know." Then the Knights' table: Robert, Diane, Felix,
Addie, Janet — the confession told plain and forgiven plain; Addie notices his
hands are cold when he passes the dish; the orchid cactus is on Felix's sill,
repotted. The window. A tall woman in a gray coat across the street, at the
exact edge of the quiet, in daylight, not hiding, and she does not look away.
He does the thing you are never supposed to do: he looks back. He does not take
it off. The last page names the Blade and where it is rumoured, and the last
line belongs to her: she knows where he is.

**EXIT — the end of Book One.**
- Necklace: on Felix. Never hides him from her again. Hides him from everyone
  else — and Book Two has other hunters.
- Folder & letters: on Felix's table; two letters sealed (Book Two, Book
  Three).
- The Hilt: under Felix's bed. Its quiet keeps her across the street. An
  ordinary man could carry it away (Armand — Book Two).
- Bike & helmets: the Walkers'.
- Injuries: Felix — cold hands, red rim; Darneil — paralyzed right side,
  permanent; the rest healing.
- Felix knows: the Blade of Tyr; Natchitoches; his mother is across the
  street and can find him anywhere; he is the protector now; he is not going
  to run.
- Addie knows: the same; she is coming to Natchitoches; she has said so before
  he asked.
- Mistress knows: where her son is. Exactly.
- Darneil knows: he told the boy what he knew; it is the boy's now.
- Janet knows: the ward will not hold; the door needs a keeper up top and it is
  her; the Blade is fifty miles north and the boy is going.
- Others: Alcide has begun carving beads. Anatole keeps the door. Armand is up
  top with a name and a car.
- Owed to Book Two: the Blade of Tyr at Natchitoches; the second letter; the
  Mistress up top with Armand as her seen face; the Hilt in a Garden District
  apartment; Felix's cold hands and red rim; Darneil half-removed; the necklace
  that cannot hide him from her.
