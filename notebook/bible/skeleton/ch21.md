### Ch21 — Practice, Interrupted
**POV:** Felix (to the moment the necklace comes off and the shadow rises),
then Darneil (past the rail). **Day/time:** D6 Tue, 5–9 pm. **Locations:** the
hold; the rail; the river way.

**ENTRY** = Ch20 EXIT.

**Purpose:** The set piece. Armand's men cross the rail — six ordinary men
against a gaunt man, a boy with a bad knee, two women, and a hilt. They take
Addie and Janet by numbers and drag them past the rail toward the lamps. Felix
takes the necklace off — on purpose, his own hand, the point of the scene —
and steps past the rail to reach the shadow. It does not come; he needs it; it
smells that. Then it comes anyway, late and badly, up off the wet brick, and his
eyes go red, and she SEES him — the first time in fourteen years — and stops
where she stands. Darneil goes past the rail for his son with the knife and
takes the arrow meant to hold the boy still (the holding). Shadow on shadow:
Felix shoves, hers *hesitates* — it is his too — and in the hesitation he lays
the not-being-seen over Addie and she walks out of Armand's hands unseen. Ti-Jean
and the cistern four come up the river way behind Armand's men with Anatole's
people. The fight breaks. She withdraws to the far lamps with Armand: "Come to
me, baby. You know the way now." Darneil dragged back inside the rail, his right
side already gone.

**EXIT**
- Necklace: OFF Felix. In his right fist, then his jeans pocket. He is seen.
- Folder & letters: in the hold.
- Bike & helmets: in the lot.
- Injuries: Felix — knee; eyes red, cooling; hands cold and staying cold; a cut
  across the left palm from the rail. Addie — a scratch on the forearm from an
  arrow's dressed head, minutes of slowing, thick-tongued, coming back by
  midnight, the hand numb for days. Janet — a black eye, a cracked rib. Darneil
  — the holding, in the right shoulder; right arm and leg dead; speech thick;
  awake; inside the rail on the cot. Armand's men — two down and left; four
  fled with him. Anatole freed by Ti-Jean during the fight; on his feet, badly.
- Positions: the hold — Darneil, Addie, Janet, Anatole, Odile, Ti-Jean, the
  Hilt on the altar. Felix at the rail, on the *far* side, the necklace in his
  pocket, looking down the river way at her lamps. The Mistress and Armand at
  the market square end with the three kept ones. The hall: Armand's
  loyalists scattering. The stair: unbarred; Alcide comes down it at 9:30.
- Felix knows: what the shadow costs; she saw him; the charm will never hide
  him from her again (Darneil told him so, thick-tongued, on the cot); he
  hid Addie with it; his father took the arrow for him; he is going to her,
  now, because the three are still hers and because she is his mother and he
  has to hear it from her.
- Addie knows: he took it off for her; she walked out of a man's hands with
  nobody looking at her; her hand is numb; he is going out and she cannot
  stop him and she is going to try anyway.
- Mistress knows: his face. His eyes, which are hers. That he can do it. That
  he will come to her tonight. That the Delvaux man is finished as a
  protector and the Hilt has no hand to hold it.
- Darneil knows: he cannot hold the Hilt; his right hand is dead; there is one
  other hand of the line in this room; the boy took it off himself, which
  means the boy decides now.
- Janet knows: Alcide is on the stair; the door is open; they can get Darneil
  up if they can carry him four hundred yards and ninety-four steps.
- Others: Anatole has his hall back and half his people hurt. Alcide is coming
  down with a flashlight and a crowbar and his heart going like a fist on a
  door.
