### Ch20 — The Brother's Gambit
**POV:** Armand (underworld). **Day/time:** D6 Tue, 9 am–4 pm. **Locations:**
the shrouded room; the old street; the hall; the foot of the bell-stair; the
river way to the rail.

**ENTRY** = Ch19 EXIT.

**Purpose:** The brother moves. His grievance (born in the dark, the sun, the
sunglasses); her offer (the town is his; the door is his; up top with a name);
her plan (with the town hers she walks the old street; with the door hers she
rents a unit in Building C and comes and goes in daylight, and the rail is
reached from the town's side only, so the Delvaux man is a man in a box she now
owns). He takes the hall — Anatole bound; the ones who resist locked in the
cistern room. He takes the stair. She walks the old street for the first time
in anyone's memory and the town's people turn their faces to the wall. Her men
at the river way. The stakes made whole.

**EXIT — the end of Act III. Complete state.**
- Necklace: on Felix, in the hold.
- Folder & letters: in the hold, on the altar step (papers; opened first
  letter; two sealed).
- Bike & helmets: in the lot. Alcide's truck beside it; Alcide in the
  basement at the wooden door, which is now barred from below by Armand's men
  — he cannot get down; he has a crowbar and a bad heart.
- Injuries: Felix's knee (walking on it); Addie's wrist (fine); Anatole beaten
  — a split scalp, two broken fingers, tied in his own hall; Odile bruised;
  Ti-Jean loose in the cisterns; Darneil unhurt; Janet unhurt.
- Positions: in the hold behind the rail — Darneil, Felix, Addie, Janet, the
  Hilt. At the rail's far side, in the river way — Armand and six men with
  lamps and cudgels; the Mistress behind them with the bow and the belt and
  the three kept ones on stretchers. In the hall — Anatole tied; Armand's
  loyalists (eight); the town's people cowed. In the cisterns — Ti-Jean and
  four who got away. At the stair — two of Armand's men. At the wooden door,
  above — Alcide.
- Felix knows: the town has fallen to Armand; she is in the river way; the
  rail stops her but not Armand's men; she wants him past the rail with it
  off; the three are the price; Addie is the other price ("You I can see").
- Addie knows: the same; she feels her coming the way you feel a train through
  a floor; she has told Felix if he goes out she goes with him.
- Mistress knows: the town is hers; the door is hers; Alcide is above it with
  a crowbar (Armand's men); the Delvaux man will come past the rail for the
  boy the moment the boy is in danger — she has watched him at the rail for a
  night and knows it; the arrow is dressed with the holding.
- Darneil knows: Armand's men can cross; the Hilt binds only what he holds it
  against; he has a knife he knows is useless; he will go past the rail if the
  boy does, and she is counting on it.
- Janet knows: Anatole is alive (Ti-Jean came along the cistern channel and
  said so); the radio is dead this deep; if they get up, Alcide's truck is at
  the lot.
- Others: Anatole, bound, has told Odile where the hall's second way out is.
  Ti-Jean is going for it. Alcide is praying, which he had not done in forty
  years.
- Weather (above): clear, cold, bright; the river road dry.
