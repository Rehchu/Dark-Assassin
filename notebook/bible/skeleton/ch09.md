### Ch09 — The Unlocked Basement
**POV:** Addie. **Day/time:** D3 Sat, 3:30–6 pm. **Locations:** the Building C
basement (concrete over brick, a water heater, a smell of sage); the wooden
door; the stair beyond, glimpsed; the lot at dusk.

**ENTRY** = Ch08 EXIT.

**Purpose:** The door that does not belong: cypress, old, oiled from the far
side, in a concrete wall. Sage and something sweet at the sill. They open it a
hand's width; a stair going down, brick then stone, cold air, river smell; a
sound below — not water. Addie feels sick (weather). They close it. Addie says
tomorrow, with lights. Felix says no. He would rather not know. Dusk; wind
rising; the bike.
