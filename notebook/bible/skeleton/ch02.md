### Ch02 — Addie's Mom
**POV:** Addie. **Day/time:** D1 Thu, 5:50–6:15 pm. **Locations:** the Walkers'
stairs and front hall; the street; Jackson Street; the Ugly Mug curb.

**ENTRY**
- Necklace: sealed in its wax envelope, in the folder, in Felix's hand at Ugly
  Mug (he walked from his apartment with it; he is already there).
- Folder & letters: with Felix, unopened at the table.
- Bike & helmets: Addie's bike in the Walkers' drive; her helmet and keys in
  her hand (she went up for them at the end of Ch1 and is now coming down);
  the spare helmet strapped behind the seat as always.
- Injuries: none.
- Felix knows: he found papers headed ALEXANDRIA ORPHANAGE; there are
  envelopes he cannot read; Addie said the orphanage is apartments now on the
  east side; Addie said bring the folder and read no more.
- Addie knows: Felix is adopted; the orphanage became apartments off the river
  road; her mother mentioned it once; "this is the last ordinary night."
- Mistress knows: the light has been up and moving since Saturday; the warmth
  pools on a Garden District block and at a coffee shop on Third every
  morning; she cannot see a face; she can see the copper-haired girl on the
  bike who is always beside the nothing; she followed the girl home Tuesday;
  she has been hired at Ugly Mug for the six-to-close shift starting tonight.
- Darneil knows: nothing new. The hold. Fourteen years.
- Janet knows: Diane Knight called Sunday, upset, to say the box had gone to
  Felix; she has been waiting five days for this; she felt the cold on the
  street at dusk.
- Others: Alcide knows nothing yet. Anatole knows nothing yet. Armand has been
  told a boy will come asking about the old orphanage.

**Purpose:** Plant Janet — the errand that is a ward, the warning that is a
map — and put the Mistress across the street from the one thing near Felix she
can see. Reconcile the sun (it set an hour ago; Janet says so; Addie says it
anyway). Get Addie to the curb with the drink in her hand.

**EXIT**
- Necklace: sealed in its envelope in the folder on the table in front of
  Felix at Ugly Mug.
- Folder & letters: on the table, unopened.
- Bike & helmets: Addie's bike at the Ugly Mug curb; her helmet under her arm;
  spare on the bike.
- Injuries: none.
- Felix knows: as entry; Addie is here; he ordered her drink right.
- Addie knows: as entry, plus: her mother wants sage, butter and honey and gave
  her cash; there is "a string of missing persons cases lately"; someone was
  across the street and then was not, and it made her sick.
- Mistress knows: as entry, plus: the girl left the house in a hurry with a
  helmet at 5:55; she is on her way to the shop by the culvert and the drains.
- Darneil knows: as entry.
- Janet knows: as entry, plus: Addie is meeting Felix about "some news"; Janet
  has put it together; she is going to scrub the kitchen and then drive to
  Main Street.
- Others: as entry.
