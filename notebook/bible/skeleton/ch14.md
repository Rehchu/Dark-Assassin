### Ch14 — The Leader and His Brother
**POV:** Felix. **Day/time:** D4 Sun, 8 pm–midnight. **Locations:** the hall
(the warehouse vault): the long table, the lamps, the forty faces.

**ENTRY** = Ch13 EXIT.

**Purpose:** Anatole and Armand, in one room, disagreeing in front of their
people. The town: who they are, in their own words, and "We live here." The
truce, all five terms, said like a lease. The folder returned unopened. "Your
father is here." Where the Mistress sits in the order. Armand: "The truce is
the truce. They came down the door. They're presented." Anatole cannot refuse
without breaking it in front of everyone. He sends Ti-Jean to the rail and lets
Armand take them.
