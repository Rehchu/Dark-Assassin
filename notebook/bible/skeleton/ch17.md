### Ch17 — What Janet Knew
**POV:** Janet (first half, up top), then Addie (second half, below).
**Day/time:** D5 Mon, 6 am–1 pm. **Locations:** the dispatch center; the
Walkers' house (the closet map; the garden — she repots the orchid cactus with
her coat still on); Alcide's back door at 8 am; the Riverbend lot (the bike);
the door; the stair; the hall.

**ENTRY** = Ch16 EXIT.

**Purpose:** What Janet knew, in dispatch order: the orphanage; the man in the
rain; choosing the Knights; the box; the ward; her grandmother born under the
city; the map with two columns (DEAD — random, cardiac, one every few months
for fourteen years, forty-one; MISSING — four in six weeks, new, and she does
not know why); the church youth trip where she put a copper-haired girl and a
boy with his hair over his ears in the same van. Then Addie's POV as her mother
walks into the hall: the confrontation; "You feel it too. I hoped you
wouldn't."; "You said before the sun set."; Addie's stake sharpens — was any of
it hers? ("The rest was yours. All of it.") The missing-persons thread pays off
as a map, not yet as faces.

**EXIT**
- Necklace: on Felix.
- Folder & letters: in Addie's backpack.
- Bike & helmets: in the lot; Janet left it and locked her car beside it.
- Injuries: Felix's knee; Addie's wrist.

  Addie's mother; Janet has watched him his whole life.
- Addie knows: all of it; her mother's grandmother was born on this street;
  the dead are hers (the Mistress's); the missing are something new; the
  church van.
- Mistress knows: the Walker woman came down the door at 9 am (Armand's men).
- Darneil knows: Anatole is bringing the boy, the girl and Janet by the river

  Addie may not forgive her.

  that he will come to the door if she calls. Armand is with the Mistress.
