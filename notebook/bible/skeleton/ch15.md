### Ch15 — The Shrouded Room
**POV:** Addie. **Day/time:** D5 Mon, 12:30–2:30 am. **Locations:** the far end
of the old street; the market square; a fissure down; the shrouded room; back
up to the hall.

**ENTRY** = Ch14 EXIT.

**Purpose:** The Mistress in the flesh. The cloth, the candle, the low table,
the brass box. She looks at Felix and her eye slides off him; she looks at
Addie and does not blink. "Back again are we." The apron is gone and the name
tag is gone and it is the same lady with the black hair — Addie's dawning. Her
hair moves; Addie sees the ear. Felix has gone white: he knows the voice — "I
said I'd hear you." He does not say what she said. "You I can see just fine."
She does not take him; she cannot see him plainly enough to bind him and she
does not want him dead; she wants him to come. She sends them back up with
Armand. Armand is furious and obeys.

**EXIT**
- Necklace: on Felix.
- Folder & letters: in Addie's backpack. She saw her own mark on the envelopes
  and said nothing.
- Bike & helmets: in the lot.
- Injuries: Felix's knee; Addie's wrist.
- Felix knows: the woman under the city is the waitress; she whispered to him
  on Friday and has just said the same words in the dark; the craftsman said
  "you are hers"; he has not told Addie what was whispered and now cannot
  without telling her everything.
- Addie knows: the waitress; the ear under the hair — the same ear as Felix's;
  "You I can see just fine" was a promise; Felix is keeping something from
  her and it is about the whisper.
- Mistress knows: at arm's length, in her own room, she could not hold his
  face; the charm is Alcide's best work; the boy knew her voice; the girl is a
  Walker and can be seen; Armand can be pushed one more step.
- Darneil knows: Ti-Jean came back: "She let them go." He has sat down for the
  first time since seven.
- Janet knows: on shift until 7.
- Others: Anatole has them back in the hall at 2:30 and has posted his own
  people at the hall door against his brother.
