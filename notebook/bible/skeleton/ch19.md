### Ch19 — Red Eyes
**POV:** the Mistress (first half — the river way to the rail, the kept ones
carried), then Felix (from inside the quiet). **Day/time:** D5 Mon 9 pm – D6
Tue 1 am. **Locations:** the river way beyond the rail; the hold.

**ENTRY** = Ch18 EXIT.

**Purpose:** Her power on the page, all of it, at the one place it stops: the
shadow rising along four hundred yards of brick and going flat and still at an
iron rail like water at a sill. The red. The kept ones — three alive on
stretchers, held, eyes open; one she takes in front of him, with the red, to
show him what the taking is. She names Darneil at the rail ("Someone clever.
Someone close.") — the only time she says his name. The bargain: come out past
the rail with it off and these three go home; stay and they do not. She knows
her son; she says so. He does not come out. She withdraws with the three. "Not
long, baby."

**EXIT**
- Necklace: on Felix.
- Folder & letters: in the hold.
- Bike & helmets: in the lot.
- Injuries: Felix's knee; Addie's wrist. Darneil unhurt. One kept one dead on
  the river way; three alive, hers.
- Felix knows: what the taking looks like; the missing are alive and hers; the
  price she has named; the rail is the only reason any of them are alive; the
  whisper was an invitation to exactly this.
- Addie knows: the same; the faces of the three (one is on her mother's map);
  Felix is going to do it — she saw it in him before he did.
- Mistress knows: the rail is the rail; the boy will come out for the three,
  or for the girl; Armand and six men are hers; Anatole is alone in his hall;
  tomorrow she takes the town.
- Darneil knows: she will not wait; Armand has men; the rail stops her and
  nothing else.
- Janet knows: Alcide is at the door with the truck from midnight; she has a
  radio he cannot hear.
- Others: Anatole is barricading the hall with Odile and Ti-Jean and a dozen
  who are not afraid of Armand yet.
