### Ch23 — The Hilt Decided
**POV:** Felix (the hold, the passing), then Darneil (the stair, the sky, the
hospital bed). **Day/time:** D7 Wed, 7 am–7 pm. **Locations:** the hold; the
river way; the hall; the bell-stair; the basement; the lot (the bike, the
truck); the river road; Rapides Regional; Felix's apartment.

**ENTRY** = Ch22 EXIT.

**Purpose:** Custody. Darneil cannot hold it; there is one hand of the line in
the room; the oath, three plain sentences, said by a boy with cold hands. The
Hilt goes still in his grip like a thing that has decided. Anatole's town
counted. Up: Darneil carried four hundred yards and ninety-four steps by people
who were afraid of him for fourteen years. The sky — Darneil's POV, the first
light in fourteen years, in a Riverbend parking lot on a cold bright morning,
and he weeps once, hard. The three kept ones left on a bench by the river road
for a passing car to find. Rapides Regional: "stroke." Felix's apartment: the
Hilt in the Knights' box, under the bed. Janet scrubs the kitchen and burns
sage at the sills. The price paid: a father who will not walk again, a mother
who knows.

**EXIT**
- Necklace: on Felix. Ring on the same cord.
- Folder & letters: on Felix's kitchen table. Papers; the opened first letter;
  two sealed.
- The Hilt: in the Knights' box under Felix's bed, in a warded apartment, in
  the Garden District. Its quiet reaches the street.
- Bike & helmets: Addie rode the bike home from the lot at noon; helmets in
  the Walkers' hall.
- Injuries: Felix — knee healing; palm bandaged; hands cold; red rim in low
  light. Addie — numb right hand; the scratch scabbed. Janet — rib strapped,
  black eye going yellow. Darneil — Rapides Regional, room 412, right side
  paralyzed, speech thick and clearing, admitted under his own name for the
  first time since he was thirty.
- Felix knows: he is the protector; the oath; the Hilt keeps her a stone's
  throw off; Darneil is in a hospital bed a mile from the Knights; tomorrow is
  Thanksgiving and Diane has called nine times.
- Addie knows: he took an oath she heard; her mother and Alcide have a plan
  for the door that does not include her, and she has told them it does.
- Mistress knows: the Hilt went up the door at 9:14 am in a boy's two hands;
  where the boy sleeps; where the Delvaux man lies; that she can walk the old
  street and the door is Anatole's again and it does not matter.
- Darneil knows: the boy has it; the boy said the words; his own right hand
  is gone and his son's are cold.
- Janet knows: the apartment is warded; it will not hold her, only slow her;
  the three kept ones are on the six o'clock news as "found alive, confused,"
  and the map has three fewer names.
- Others: Alcide drove and did not speak the whole way and, at the hospital
  door, said "Jan" once. Anatole has fourteen people who will not look at him
  and twenty-six who will. Armand is up top with her, in a gray car nobody
  remembers.
