### Ch12 — The Door Goes Down
**POV:** Felix. **Day/time:** D4 Sun, 2:30–4:30 pm. **Locations:** Felix's
apartment; the river road (flooding at the low place — the grate); the
Riverbend lot; the basement; the wooden door; the bell-stair.

**ENTRY** = Ch11 EXIT.

**Purpose:** Heavy rain. He gets on the bike without a word (plant 26). The
low place on the river road is a black sheet running for the grate. Addie's
field kit: headlamps, rope, the hand lens she always forgets and this time
does not. The door. Ninety-four steps down. Brick, then stone. The city built
on top of something older — the first sight of the buried street, a cypress
lintel, a shopfront with a name still on it. Felix's history-love and his dread
in the same breath.

**EXIT**
- Necklace: on Felix.
- Folder & letters: in Addie's backpack, on her back.
- Bike & helmets: bike in the Riverbend lot, Building C side, in the rain;
  helmets locked to it. It stays there until Wednesday.
- Injuries: none.
- Felix knows: the stair is real; there is a street down here; nobody up top
  knows; his phone has one bar at the top and none at the bottom.
- Addie knows: the sage at the foot of the stair, fresh; she feels sick and
  worse the further along the street they go; she is not going back up
  without knowing.
- Mistress knows: the door opened at 3:50 and stayed open; the warmth is on the
  old street, over her.
- Darneil knows: as Ch11; waiting at the rail.
- Janet knows: woke at 3 to the note; drove to the lot in the rain at 3:40;
  found the bike; the MAINTENANCE door; stood in the basement; the wooden
  door shut; she did not open it — she is on shift at 7 and she knows Anatole
  has them. She went home and did not sleep.
- Others: Anatole's watchers have them in sight. Armand's men are coming along
  the old street from the other end.
