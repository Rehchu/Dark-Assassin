### Ch10 — Practice
**POV:** the Mistress. **Day/time:** D3 Sat night into D4 Sun, 1–3:30 am.
**Locations:** the shrouded room; the second chamber (the kept ones, passed,
not shown in full); the fissures; the river-road drain; the river road under
the levee; a Garden District block; back.

**ENTRY** = Ch09 EXIT.

**Purpose:** The dread chapter. She practices — one taking, a person of the
writer's choosing at the river road, found Sunday morning, "cardiac." She
thinks about the week: the warmth on Saturday, the girl, the shop, the boy's
shape at the table when the stone left his neck, his block with no door, the
Walker house that smells of sage, the door opened a hand's width over her head
this afternoon. She stands at the edge of his block again and cannot see a
door. Rain begins. Armand comes to her (she sent for him): "Let them come down.
Take them to me, not to your brother. Do not touch the girl." The randoms
thread is on the page.
