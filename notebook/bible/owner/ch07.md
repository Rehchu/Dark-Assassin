### 07 — The Man Who Made The Necklace (extension rules, not preservation)

Every line of the existing `07` stands. Three insertion points are *required*
by this bible, all additive:

- **Between line 22 ("You keep it on.") and line 24 ("Felix touched the beads
  at his throat"):** Alcide pushes the necklace back across the felt and makes
  Felix put it on before he says another word. Without this beat line 24 has
  the necklace on the felt and at Felix's throat at once.
- **Line 24, "back on at the coffee shop, joking … this morning":** this is
  *Friday*, Chapter 5 — Addie returned it late morning and he put it on
  joking again. The expansion of Ch5 must show that second joke so the line is
  true.
- **Between line 60 and line 62 (the flinch at *mom*):** may be extended with
  Felix's private beat; the flinch itself stands.
- **After line 74 (Alcide looking out at Main Street):** may be extended so
  that Felix follows his look and sees nothing but a doorway across the street
  and a shape in it that is gone when he blinks. Optional; recommended.
