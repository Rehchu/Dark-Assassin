
Addie Griffiths and Felix Knight, have been dating since their Freshman year of high school. Addie recently finishes her first Semester of College, and Felix is moving into a new apartment. Felix was going through some of his things and finds some adoption papers.
 They get curious and eventually go check out where he was adopted from. They go into an orphanage converted into a new apartment complex, and stumble upon a basement that wasn't locked.
	Curiosity catches their eye, and they enter the basement. They see a strange wooden door that just looks out of place and go check it out. It goes down further and further. until they reach what looks like ruins of an older civilization as if the city was just built right on top.
	They are followed by "leader of underground ruins" and are introduced to his brother, and "Mistress"
The mistress turns out to be an Assassin, and sneaks to the overworld and "practice her skills", which the local police believe are just random murders. she has special powers that are passed down through her bloodline.
	Addie and Felix have to find a way to stop her before she ends up overthrowing the leader and taking over the overworld. They discover Felix is part of the bloodline of the assassins, and utilize his newfound abilities to help stop the bad guy"
