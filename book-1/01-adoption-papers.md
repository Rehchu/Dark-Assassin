# Adoption Papers


It was a cool fall evening, and Addie Walker had just finished her research paper for her experimental methodology class before the Thanksgiving break. She was so excited to be able to get out of the house and hit the hiking trails this fall.  Addie loves the outdoors, and loves nature.  She had books on bird watching, the different flora and fauna in her local area. She happily rushed down the stairs to relieve her work stress in her garden, and started replanting her Bonsai trees and refilling her bird feeders.
She started to repot her five year old orchid cactus that her boyfriend, Felix knight, had gifted her during his trip to Belmopan, Belize. Felix enjoys to travel the world, and is a renowned travel blogger. Felix was moving into a new apartment after his recent travel in Belfast.
As Addie was washing her hands after her time in the garden,  she received a call from Felix “Hello”? Said Addie immediately upon answering. “How’s moving into your new apartment coming along?” “It is starting to come together nicely, just a few more touches, and it’ll feel just like home.” Responded  Felix. “I called because I have some news I don’t like the feeling about” said Felix in an urgent tone. “I was going through some of my old things, and found some adoption papers. I think they’re mine.” A moment of silence was left between them. Addie continues to ask “Do you know that they’re yours?” “Do you think your parents would have informed you about something like this?”
Felix stops to think for a moment.  He flips through the pages and looks at the information.  It says in bold font: 
ALEXANDRIA ORPHANAGE.  
“Do we even have an orphanage in Alexandria?” Asks Felix. “I don’t think we do anymore.” “I think it had gotten redeveloped, and converted into a new apartment complex.” States Addie. “ Do you want to meet up at Ugly Mug Coffee?” Asks Felix “I think we need to discuss this more in person, and take a walk downtown to help clear my mind.”  “I’ll meet you there.” Says Addie, and she hangs up the phone.
