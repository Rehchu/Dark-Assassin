# Research


Addie rushed to the store to retrieve the items her mom asked for. Immediately, she realized she had Felix’s necklace. She grabbed the items she needed, and headed home. When she pulled up to the house, her mom’s car was gone. “I wonder where she went,” she thought.
The bag is brought into the kitchen, and placed on the table. She could tell it had just gotten cleaned. The counters were sparkling, the floors looked like it could be eaten off of. Addie grabs a snack out of the fridge, and heads up the stairs.  Immediately after arriving in her room, she locks the door, opens her computer, and starts looking up “Black Rocks” on the internet.
There was a ton of information relating to black stones and different rocks.  She pulled up a picture, and placed the rock next to it.  “AH HA!  This looks like it could be a piece of Obsidian,” she exclaimed with joy.  “The letter said to never take it off.  I wonder why that is.”  She decided to dig a little deeper, and see what could be the reasoning.  According to the research she found on the internet, “Obsidian is used as protection against negative energies, and blocks psychic attacks.”  She wondered “why would there be a note that says do not take this off?” “Is someone after him?” Her mind kept racing.  Hundreds of thoughts kept coming across her mind, and she could not find an answer.
She kept examining the piece of Obsidian.  There was a faint engraving on it that almost resembling a fork.  Addie kept digging into the research and discovered that it was an ancient rune used as protection against enemies.  “Now why would you need a stone that protected against negative energies, and a carving that allegedly protects you against enemies,” she wondered.  This was all too strange for Addie, and she didn’t know of anyone that would have any answers.
