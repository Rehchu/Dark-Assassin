# The Coffee Shop (part 1)


Addie and Felix sit at the next available table. She is still curious as to what is going on with.  “Why hasn’t he said anything about the adoption?” “Why is he waiting?” Addie thought to herself.  With her mind puzzled, she kept running through every scenario in her head as to why his parents never told him.   “How’s the drink? Did they make it right?” Asked Felix.  Addie shook her head up and down, and gave the biggest, goofiest grin to show her appreciation.  “It’s absolutely fantastic.” Addie says.
A tall, black haired, dark complexioned waitress with a flour coated apron came up to the table.  “Is there anything I can get for you guys today?” the waitress continued to ask “Do you need any refills or pastries?” Felix politely declined the offer.  Addie’s stomach started to feel like it was being twisted into knots.  “Do I know you?” She asks “are you just coming on your shift?” The waitress promptly replies “I just started today.  I’m still learning the ropes, and just want to make sure everyone is satisfied with my performance.”
As the waitress walks away to another table, Felix pulls out a folder full of paperwork.  “This is why I wanted you to come.  Look at all of this.” Felix said with a worried tone.  “There are more than just adoption papers in here.” “I found some letters.  They appear to be written in a foreign language, but I don’t recognize it.” Addie reaches for one of the envelopes in the folder.  It’s sealed with a red wax ring.  “What’s this?” She asks Felix as she inspects it further.  “It looks like there’s something inside.”
 Felix snatches the envelope out of Addie’s hands, and opens it with caution.  He reaches inside, and pulls out a necklace.  “This is peculiar, I’ve never seen anything like this before.” It looked like a black rock with some sort of symbol on it.  “Is there anything else in there?” Addie asks Felix as she continues going through the different notes.  “It looks like there is a note with it.” “It just says: 
do not take this off under any circumstance.  Keep it on you at all times.”
Giving Addie strange looks, he decides to put on the necklace. “How does it look?” He asks jokingly.  The waitress comes back to the table, and checks up on everyone, and notices Felix’s necklace.  “What a lovely stone, wherever did you get it?” the waitress asked. “I was just going through some old things, and there it was.” The two were finishing up their drinks, and Addie remembered she needed to run to the store for her mom.
