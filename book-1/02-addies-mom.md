# Addies Mom


Addie headed down the stairs when she was immediately stopped by her mom, Janet.  “Where do you think you’re going in such a cheerful mood?” Asks her mom.  “I’m headed to meet up with Felix at Ugly Mug, and talk about some news he recently found out.” Replies Addie.  “I won’t be long! I’m just going to be gone enough to talk this through, then I’ll be back before the sun sets.” “While you’re out then, can you pick up some sage, butter, and honey?”  “I’ll give you a few dollars to pick it all up, and you can use what’s left to buy you something to drink.” Says Janet. “Be careful, there have been a string of missing persons cases lately .”  Addie grabs the cash from her mom, gives her a hug, and rushes out the door in an instant.
As she makes her way to her bike, she notices someone across the street.  She gets this gut wrenched feeling, almost like she is going to be sick. She looks back across the street, and in that moment, they were gone.  Addie was in a rush to meet up with Felix, so she didn’t take much note of it. She jumped on her bike, revved the engine, and before you know it, she went on her way.
Addie was feeling ambitious.  She decided to zip in and out of lanes, as she loved performing fearless lane splitting stunts, dodging every car that she came up on.  She could see it in the horizon, the coffee shop.  Upon arrival, Addie takes her helmet off, and runs up to Felix. “I ordered for you already.” He said as Addie walked up.  “Iced white chocolate mocha with an extra shot, and I made sure they didn’t put any whipped cream on it.” Felix hands the drink to Addie, and she takes that first sip and savors the moment as if it’s the last time she’ll ever have a drink.
