# The Pawn Shop


Felix and Addie sped outside and jumped on Addie’s bike.  They had no idea what they were going to uncover.  They knew they had to figure out where the necklace came from, and why nobody told Felix what was going on. “Put this on” said Addie as she was giving Felix a spare helmet.  “This might get a little wild” Addie revved up her bike’s engine, and took off. Felix immediately grabbed her around the waist before he could fall off.  She had no regard for her own safety nor Felix’s.  The only thing she had on her mind was getting to the pawn shop.
 Left turn, right turn, zipping in and out of traffic.  She was being reckless, and Felix  terrified.  He grabbed her tighter, and tried to get her to stop.  Before he knew it, they had arrived. 
GOLD AND SILVER PAWN SHOP 
had displayed vertically on a sign attached to the building.  “I never want to be on that thing again” said Felix while he was trying not to be sick. They checked out the building.  It was quaint, and it seemed to have seen better days.  “Are they even open”? Asked Addie.  “I hope I didn’t waste a trip over here for nothing”.  	A figure in the window moves, and makes Addie jump. “I think they’re open.  I see someone inside” responded Felix. He walks inside, and notices trinkets, books, clothes, and even a little unicorn mobile hanging.  Before either one of them could say anything, a man approaches from behind a curtain and heads to the counter. “Can I help you with anything?” He asks.  Felix and Addie both approach with caution.  “We are told that you might be able to help us with this” Felix hands the necklace over to the shopkeeper.  He examines the string upon which the necklace hangs. He begins to individually inspect every hand crafted wooden bead, then finally the  obsidian for which the rune is engraved.  He stared at it for a brief time, almost as if he was mesmerized by it.
“Wherever did you get this?” The man asked.  Addie and Felix quickly glanced at each other, then looked back at the man.  “I’ll ask again.  Where did you receive this item from?”  He asked again, but this time with anger in his voice.  “I don’t know, it was in a box of some old stuff my parents left me when I moved out.” Said Felix as he stepped back with fear.  “Do you want to know about this necklace” the man asked.  “Of course we do! It’s why we are here!” exclaimed Addie.
